Projects we take on

Most of our residential work falls into one of these

Whole-home repaints

Every room, ceilings and trim included, done in a planned sequence so your family always has usable space. Common when you've just bought the house or finally got tired of the builder beige.

Single rooms & refreshes

A nursery before the baby comes, a home office that became a bedroom, a stairwell nobody has touched since the 90s. Small jobs get the same prep as big ones.

Getting a house ready to sell

Neutral colors, patched walls, fresh trim and a clean exterior. It's the cheapest thing you can do before listing and the first thing buyers notice in photos.

Paint before you move in

Empty house, no furniture to work around, no dust in your things. If your closing lines up with a gap in the schedule, this is the easiest version of the job for everyone.

Kitchens, baths & cabinets

Cabinet refinishing, trim work and moisture-appropriate coatings for the rooms that take the most abuse. A big change without a full remodel.

Renovations & repairs

New sheetrock, converted rooms and post-renovation finishing. If a contractor left you with fresh drywall and no paint, we can pick it up from there.

Working in a lived-in house

You shouldn't have to move out to get your house painted.

Kids, pets, work-from-home calls, deliveries. A residential job has to fit into a real week.

  • We agree on a room order up front so you always have somewhere to be
  • Consistent arrival times, and a call if anything changes
  • Doorways sealed off so dust and odor stay in the work area
  • Low-odor interior products wherever they're appropriate
  • Tools packed away and floors swept before we leave each day
  • A walkthrough at the end where you point and we fix
