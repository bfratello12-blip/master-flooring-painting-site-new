Local knowledge

Long Island houses come with a pattern.

After enough jobs out here you stop being surprised by what's behind the wall.

  • South shore. Salt air and wind accelerate everything. Exteriors chalk and fail faster, and hardware corrodes. Prep and coating choice matter more here than anywhere else on the island.
  • North shore. Heavy tree cover means shade, moisture and algae growth on siding, decks and patios. Washing usually comes before painting — sometimes instead of it.
  • Post-war capes & splits. The Levittown-era housing stock all shares the same tired trim details and undersized closets. We've painted a lot of them and know where the shortcuts were taken originally.
  • Older homes. Plaster walls, layered oil-based trim and settlement cracks all need different handling than modern drywall. Skipping that step is why cracks come back.
  • Basements. Most of them have a moisture history. Painting over it without dealing with it is a temporary fix at best.
