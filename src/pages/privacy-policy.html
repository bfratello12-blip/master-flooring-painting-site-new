Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us through this website.

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you fill out an estimate request or contact form on this site, we collect the information you choose to give us — typically your name, phone number, email address, the town where the project is located, the service you're interested in and any details you write about the job.

We do not ask for payment information through this website.

How we use it

We use the information you submit for one purpose: to respond to your request. That means contacting you to discuss the project, schedule a walkthrough, provide an estimate and follow up about the work.

By submitting a form you're agreeing that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your request. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't rent or trade your information to other companies.
  • We don't add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

Service providers

This website may use third-party services for hosting, form delivery, email and basic website analytics. Those providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function, and they are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the pages so we can improve them. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings.

Advertising

We may run advertising through platforms such as Google. If advertising or conversion tracking is active, those platforms may set cookies to measure whether an ad led to a form submission or phone call. You can manage ad personalization through your Google account settings or your browser.

Data retention

We keep estimate requests and project correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and maintain our business records. If you'd like your information removed, contact us and we'll take care of it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive personal or financial information through the website forms.

Children

This website is intended for adults hiring a contractor. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page.
