What's included

A finish that starts at the drywall

Interior paint hides nothing. Every nail pop, seam and skim-coat shadow shows up under a fresh coat — so the repairs come first.

  • Walls and ceilings. Patching, sanding, spot-priming and two full coats of quality interior paint in the sheen you choose.
  • Trim, doors and molding. Baseboards, casings, crown, closet doors and railings — sanded, caulked and coated in a durable enamel.
  • Kitchen cabinets. Degreased, sanded, primed and finished for a smooth, hard surface that stands up to real kitchen use.
  • Problem areas. Water stains sealed, old wallpaper glue handled, textured or damaged walls skimmed smooth before painting.
  • Basements, stairwells and hallways. The high-traffic spaces that usually get skipped and end up the most beat-up.
How it goes

What to expect when we're in your house

Interior work happens where you live. That shapes how we run the job.

Day one

Protect and prep

Furniture is moved to the middle of the room and covered, floors are papered or dropped, plates and hardware come off, and every repair gets made and primed before any color goes up.

The work

Cut, roll, repeat

Ceilings first, then walls, then trim. Edges are cut by hand for a clean line, and we come back for a second full coat rather than stretching one thin coat to cover.

Wrap up

Put back and walk through

Furniture returns, plates and hardware go back on, floors get swept and vacuumed. Then we walk the rooms with you in daylight and touch up anything you flag.

Color help

Not sure about the color yet? That's normal.

Half the people who call us know the exact shade they want. The other half have four sample squares on the wall and can't decide.

We'll tell you honestly how a color is going to read in your room — north-facing rooms pull cool, south-facing rooms warm everything up, and a color that looks perfect in the store can look completely different over your existing floors. We'll also talk through sheen, which matters more than most people expect: flat hides wall imperfections, eggshell and satin wipe down, and semi-gloss belongs on trim and doors that get touched constantly.
