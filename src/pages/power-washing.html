Pressure matters

Too much pressure does damage you can't undo.

A pressure washer will absolutely take dirt off vinyl siding. It will also drive water behind it, etch soft wood, blow out mortar joints and strip the surface off a paver if you use the wrong tip and stand too close.

  • Pressure, tip and distance adjusted for each material
  • Low-pressure soft washing where the surface calls for it
  • Plants, lights, outlets and furniture protected before we start
  • Consistent passes so you don't end up with stripes and missed patches
  • Rinse-down and cleanup when the job's done
